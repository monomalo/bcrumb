FAQ

What should I bring?

Water, then more water. Light clothes you don't mind soaking. Mats and towels are provided.

Do I have to laugh?

Yes. It's part of the sequence.

How hot is it?

The main room holds 105°F at 40% humidity, cooler than the street outside most afternoons.

Where do I park?

Hangar 3, Dune Flat Outpost, next to the water reclaimer. Free for members.
